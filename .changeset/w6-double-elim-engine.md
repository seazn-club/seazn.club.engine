W6 double elimination (format matrix): losers'-bracket drops that cross by the four-step cycle, a named refusal below 2 entrants, the `resetOwed` predicate with the `reset_owed` completion refusal, and places shared by losers'-bracket exit round with a completed-places check that reads relations. Design of record: seazn-club/seazn.club `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-format-matrix-w6-double-elim-design.md` (rulings 293–306); the rules are in `rules/cross-sport.md`.

- BREAKING: `generateDoubleElim` crosses the losers'-bracket drops (X-DE-4, RB6-X2, rulings 295 and 300). For every field of 5 or more, the `awayFrom` of each LB drop round names a different winners'-bracket game: `lb-r{L}-i{j}` takes the loser of `wb-r{m+1}-i{lbDropGameRB6X2(m, G, j)}` with m = (L − 1) / 2, where it took `wb-r{m+1}-i{j}`. Fields of 4 or fewer are unchanged. Fixture ids, `round` values and every other feed are unchanged. Migration: a stored bracket keeps its stored edges. Never regenerate and diff a stored double elimination's feeds, and never derive an LB seat's source from the game index: read the stored feed.
- BREAKING: `generateDoubleElim` throws `CONFIG_INVALID` `{ reason: "double_elim_needs_two", entrants }` below 2 entrants (X-DE-16). Before, it returned no fixture at all. `generateSingleElim` is unchanged. Migration: size-check before generating, and answer 0 lines below 2.
- BREAKING: a double elimination's places are shared by losers'-bracket exit round (`bracketRanks`, `completeBracketStage`'s `finalRanks`; X-FR-6 … X-FR-8; rulings 297, 303, 305(d), 306(c)). Entrants out in one LB round share one rank, 1 + entrants placed above them (5 (shared), 7 (shared), 9 (shared) …); a winners'-bracket withdrawal ranks at the LB round of the seat it vacates. LB rounds are compared only with LB rounds, so per-lane rounds rank the same as the generator's. Before: strict by raw round, then seed, then id. `FinalPlace` is unchanged; `BRACKET_SHARED_RANKS` keeps its value and now bounds knockout, stepladder and page playoff only. Migration: any reader of `FinalPlace[]` must accept a tie of any size at any rank below the title, and a skipped rank.
- BREAKING (meaning): in a double elimination `BracketFixture.walkedOver: "loser"` means the loser is out of the stage there (X-DE-1 amended, X-FR-3, RB6-F1, F4). A one-loss forfeit, award or retirement written with it now ranks as a withdrawal, at the vacated LB round, not by the second loss. Migration: write a one-loss forfeit or retirement as a plain loss (winner, loser, no `walkedOver`), and keep `walkedOver: "loser"` for withdrawn or disqualified entrants.
- BREAKING: the completed-places check for a double elimination is different (`assertCompletedBracketPlaces`, `completeBracketStage`; X-FR-9). "At most two" and `BRACKET_SHARED_RANKS` no longer apply to it. After the structural check it refuses, in this order, each `CONFIG_INVALID` with `stageId`: `lane_missing` (`fixtureId`: a row with no `bracket`), `exit_round_unreadable` (`entrantId`, `fixtureId`), `title_shape` (`title`: `champion` | `joint` | `vacant` | `none`, `rankOne`), `exit_round_order` (`entrantId`, `rank`, `laterEntrantId`, `laterRank`), `exit_round_split` (`entrantId`, `rank`, `sameRoundEntrantId`, `sameRoundRank`), `not_competition_ranking`. Migration: pass `bracket` on every double-elimination row, and the generator's feeds on every row (the WB loser feeds above all).
- BREAKING: `not_competition_ranking` now has TWO data shapes. For a double elimination it carries `{ stageId, entrantId, rank, expected }`; for knockout, stepladder and page playoff it still carries `{ stageId, rank, holders }`. Migration: a reader that formats this refusal branches on the stage kind, or reads only `reason`.
- BREAKING: `completeBracketStage` refuses a void reset that the losers' champion earned (X-DE-12, ruling 298): it throws `STAGE_NOT_READY` `{ stageId, reason: "reset_owed", reset, grandFinal }`, checked after `no_final` and `fixtures_undecided`, and `isBracketStageComplete` is false. It applies only to rows that carry the new `BracketFixture.conditional`: a consumer still sending rows without it never sees `reset_owed`. Migration: set `conditional` on the reset row, decide owed or not with `resetOwed`, and never void a reset that is owed.
- BREAKING: `bracketTitle` waits for an owed reset (X-DE-11). With `conditional` set and the reset owed, the deciding match is the reset, and the title is undefined until the reset decides something. Before, GF1's winner was the title when the reset was void. Migration: read the title from `bracketTitle` or `completeBracketStage`, never from GF1's winner.
- BREAKING: new throws on reset wiring. When a `conditional` row exists, `resetOwed`, `bracketTitle`, `bracketRanks`, `isBracketStageComplete` and `completeBracketStage` can throw `CONFIG_INVALID` `reset_not_unique` (`fixtureIds`), `reset_feed_invalid` (`fixtureId`) or `grand_final_feed_invalid` (`fixtureId`); these carry no `stageId`. Migration: attach `homeFrom` / `awayFrom` to the reset and to GF1, and `bracket` to GF1's two source rows.
- BREAKING (testkit): `simulateDivision`'s double-elimination outcomes change: crossed drops, places shared by exit round, and the reset is played only when `resetOwed` (a GF1 walkover with no loser now voids it). Migration: re-record any pinned double-elimination simulation output or replay expectation.
- New: `lbDropGameRB6X2(m, games, i)` (`/scheduling`): the 0-based LB game that takes the loser of 0-based WB game `i` in the m-th drop after WB round 1. Refuses `CONFIG_INVALID` `drop_game_invalid` (`m`, `games`, `i`). A stored bracket is its stored edges: do not call it to interpret stored rows.
- New: `resetOwed(fixtures)` (`/competition`): false when no row is `conditional`; else true iff GF1 has no walkover, names a winner and a loser, its winner is what GF1's LB feed delivered and its loser what its WB feed delivered. Seat order is never read.
- New: `BracketFixture.conditional` (true on the reset row only), and the `reset_owed` `STAGE_NOT_READY` reason.
- Rules: 26 W6 rows (rulings 293–306); X-DE-1, X-FR-1, X-FR-2, X-FR-3 amended; X-DE-1's status is now 294. The 14 engine rows (X-DE-2 … X-DE-6, X-DE-10 … X-DE-12, X-DE-16, X-CH-9, X-FR-6 … X-FR-9) are proved, with their `AWAITING_PROOF` entries removed; the 12 W6 app rows await proof ("W6 app PR B").
- Stryker: no new file; legs `draws-3` (`bracket.ts`) and `competition-5` (`stage.ts`) owe a re-measure.
- No fold change: `FOLD_EPOCH` and `FOLD_MINOR` are unchanged.
